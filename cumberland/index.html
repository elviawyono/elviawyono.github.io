Cumberland Project

Scenario-based visualization for community engagement in district energy planning

Project Overview

This project explores how scenario-based landscape visualization can support community engagement around future clean energy systems in Cumberland, British Columbia.

The work focuses on communicating future possibilities through visual and spatial storytelling approaches that make energy infrastructure and planning discussions more accessible to broader audiences.

Approach

The project combines landscape visualization, scenario development, and community-oriented communication approaches to help participants explore possible future energy systems and their impacts on place.

Visualization scenarios were developed using 3D spatial modeling and environmental rendering workflows to create immersive and understandable representations of possible energy futures.

Why Visualization Matters

Clean energy transitions are not only technical challenges — they are also social and spatial questions that require communication, participation, and place-based understanding.

Scenario-based visualization can help communities:

  • See possible future conditions
  • Understand spatial implications
  • Support dialogue and reflection
  • Engage with planning discussions more meaningfully
