Cumberland Project

Scenario-based visualization for community engagement in district energy planning.

Project Overview

This project explores how scenario-based landscape visualization can help communicate possible clean energy futures in Cumberland, BC.

Why It Matters

Clean energy transitions are not only technical challenges. They are also social and spatial questions that require clear communication, community dialogue, and place-based understanding.

Approach

The project uses landscape visualization to help make energy infrastructure and future scenarios more visible, accessible, and discussable for broader audiences.
